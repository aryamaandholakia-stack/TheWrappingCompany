About us

We started with paper and ribbon, and kept going.

Gift wrapping is still the name on the door, and still the thing we are fussiest about. But a gift table needs flowers on it, and the flowers need a backdrop behind them, and the backdrop needs a room that has been thought about. One thing led to the next, and now we do all of it.

Everything is put together by hand, in materials chosen for the occasion rather than pulled off a shelf. There is no house style you have to fit into. We start from the room, the date and the people, and the flowers and paper follow from there.

The work runs from a single wrapped gift to a full venue: baby showers, eightieth birthdays, weddings, corporate hampers by the hundred, and brand activations at festivals. The scale changes; the finish does not.

Most of what we build exists for one evening and is gone by the next morning. That is the job, and it is why we would rather do fewer of them properly. For wedding season and festive orders, it is worth getting in touch early.

Nothing here is a production line.
I

Florals and arrangements

Table arrangements, entrance urns, stage flowers and everything between. Seasonal stems, sourced the morning of, conditioned and built on site so nothing arrives tired.

We work to what the market actually has that week rather than to a mood board photographed in another country, which keeps the colours honest and the cost sensible.

  • Centrepieces and long-table runs
  • Entrance and stage arrangements
  • Home and office subscriptions
II

Backdrops and balloons

Organic balloon garlands, neon and cut-out signage, dried palm and pampas. Built to be photographed from one side and stood in front of all evening.

Palettes are mixed to order rather than bought by the bag, which is the difference between a backdrop that reads as styled and one that reads as a party shop.

  • Baby showers and naming days
  • Milestone birthdays
  • Custom signage and neon
III

Weddings and ceremonies

Arches, drapes, aisles and gift tables, plus the sagan envelopes, room hampers and guest favours that go with them.

One team for the decor and the gifting means the ribbon on the favour is the ribbon on the arch. It sounds small. It is the thing people notice in photographs years later.

  • Ceremony arches and drapes
  • Mehendi, sangeet and reception
  • Favours and room hampers in volume
IV

Hampers and corporate gifting

Crates, trays and boxes built to a budget and a headcount, in your own ribbon and colours, without the finish dropping off on the three hundredth one.

We will tell you early if the number you have in mind and the look you have in mind do not meet, and what to change first.

  • Client and team gifting at scale
  • Branded ribbon, tags and seals
  • Assembly and scheduled dispatch
V

Gift wrapping

The thing we started with. Single gifts, festive runs and trousseau packing, wrapped so the paper gets folded back rather than torn.

Awkward shapes welcome. Most of what comes to us does not fit a box, which is the reason it came to us.

  • One-off gifts and small runs
  • Diwali and Christmas volume
  • Trousseau and saree packing
VI

Brand activations

Lounges, hospitality suites and festival builds, working to a brand book and a load-in window.

We have dressed a music festival between soundcheck and doors, and been out before the first act. Tight windows are the normal condition, not the exception.

  • Festival and hospitality builds
  • Product launches and press days
  • Working to brand guidelines
